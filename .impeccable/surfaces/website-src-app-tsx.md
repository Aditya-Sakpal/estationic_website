# Surface: Estationic marketing site (website/)

Mode: Persuade. A standalone Vite + React site in `website/`, deliberately separate from the Next.js console.

Audience: sales and marketing heads at Indian residential developers. Job: understand in one viewport that Estationic runs sales and marketing on autopilot: compliant ads, an auto budget allocator, an AI caller with human handoff, WhatsApp follow-ups, site visit booking and nurturing to the booking. Action: book a demo, or sign in.

Proof on hand: the product's mechanisms. Customer logos, stats and pricing are user-approved PLACEHOLDERS copied from the reference; replace before launch. At the founder's direction (25 Sep 2026) the page presents the full product, including parts not yet built in the repo; no 'Coming soon' tags.

Constraints: no em dash, never name one state's RERA (say RERA; state rules are added over time), Indian money format once real pricing lands, prefers-reduced-motion honoured, no dead controls.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet monochrome blueprint where one red box does the work. Refuses the category default: skyline photography heroes and gradient SaaS blobs.

OWN-WORLD: #FCFCFC ground, #151515 ink, grey second lines, full-height hairline rails with plus crosshairs, square 1px-bordered cards, black rectangular CTAs, isometric ink line-art machines where the only colour is the red E box. Space Grotesk display (pinned by the reference), Inter body.

STORY: A sales or marketing head sees the whole funnel handled: ads made and kept compliant, budget moved to what books, every lead called, qualified, followed up on WhatsApp, taken to a site visit and on to the booking, with their team stepping in where it matters; they book a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: centred two-line H1 ("Ads that comply. / Leads that book."), two-line sub naming the funnel, black "Book a demo" plus outline "See how it works", then a full-width isometric conveyor carrying channel tiles (Meta, Instagram, Google, WhatsApp, portals) into a machine topped by the red E box, which emits three pads rising right: Lead qualified, Site visit booked, Unit booked. Below: Marketing (4 tabbed scenes), Sales (six-station journey with a walking lead token), Autopilot vs team lanes, Compliance.

FORM: user-pinned (aoutive.framer.website), no roll; seed key: user-pinned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
